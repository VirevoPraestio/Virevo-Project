The numbers we will watch

Hours from ready to homeYours

Starting number: 5 hours, from you.

How we measure itClose
  • From the doctor saying the patient can go, to the patient leaving the building.
  • Counted for every patient, not a sample.
  • Reported every week, ward by ward.
Home by 10 AMGoal

Goal: patients paying themselves home by 10 AM, 11 at the latest. Insured patients by 1 PM.

How we measure itClose
  • The share of each day’s patients who have left by those times.
  • No starting number yet. The first week of the trial sets it.
Decided on the evening roundNew

The share of going-home decisions made at 7 PM.

Why it mattersClose
  • It shows whether the evening is really being used. That is the heart of the fix.
  • The software records it by itself, so nobody fills in a form.
Insurance approval timeNew

How long the insurer takes, in the evening and in the morning.

Why it mattersClose
  • It shows whether sending the first bill overnight is working.
  • The insurer is outside your control, so this is kept apart from your own delays.
Bed ready for the next patientNew

Time from the patient leaving to the bed being ready again.

How we measure itClose
  • Includes the wait before housekeeping is told, not only the cleaning.
  • Checked against the bed desk’s own times.
Money won backYours

Freed beds given to new patients, against the ₹10.3 crore a year at stake.

How we measure itClose
  • Only beds that are really filled again count, not beds that are just freed.
  • The new roles pay for themselves at about 1% of this.

Show me how we’ll know it worked

We’ll watch a short list of numbers, week by week. Where you already gave me a number, that is where we start. Why these:

  • They measure the wait itself, not how busy people are, so a busy week can’t hide a slow one.
  • They start from your own numbers, so progress is measured against you, not against other hospitals.
  • Nothing tracks some of them today. Those are marked new, and the first week of the trial gives their starting number.

That’s all five parts. Shall I put them into a plan, starting with a short trial?
