# Discharge Process — index card

Covers everything from the clinical discharge decision to the bed becoming available again: doctor sign-off and the summary, nursing stock-take, billing closure, insurance/TPA clearance, family briefing, payment, and physical exit. A query belongs here when it touches discharge speed or timing, Discharge TAT, ALOS attributable to process delay, or a bed that will not free up on time.
