The fix

What the fix is made of

Five parts. Open any one to see what’s in it.

  • Automatic paperwork

    Start here

    What the software does, so nobody does the work twice.

    • The summary is drafted from the notes already written, ready for the doctor to check.
    • The bill builds itself as the stay happens, so closing it is a signature and not an assembly.
    • Pharmacy returns are counted once, at the ward, and credited from that count.

  • Changes on the wards

    Part 2

    What each team does differently, step by step.

    • Nursing starts the stock-take on the evening decision, not on the morning signature.
    • Housekeeping is told the moment the bed is released, without a phone call.
    • Transport is booked against the expected hour rather than the actual one.

  • People who must agree

    Part 3

    Who has to say yes, and what each is asked to do.

    • The medical director, to let the evening round carry weight.
    • Finance, to accept a bill that closes without a final manual check.
    • The TPA desk, to raise approvals the night before.

  • Who is in charge

    Part 4

    Whether you need someone new to run this.

    • One discharge coordinator per shift, not per ward.
    • It can be an existing role with the time protected, if the wards run under 80%.
    • Above that, your own numbers say it needs to be someone’s whole job.

  • How we’ll know it worked

    Part 5

    The few numbers we will watch, week by week.

    • Hours from the decision to the patient actually leaving.
    • The share of patients out before 2 PM.
    • Hours a bed sits empty between two patients.
