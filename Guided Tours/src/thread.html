Discharges are slipping past 2pm on Ward 4 — where is it going wrong?
TTojoChat · reading Discharge references

Ward 4 isn't slow at the end. Its discharge starts too late.

Today the work starts only when the doctor says yes. Then the summary is typed, supplies are counted and the bill is made. The insurer gets the bill last, so it gets the blame.

Three things keep it that way:

  1. The bill and the summary are made after the decision, not built day by day.
  2. Medicines and supplies are counted by hand before the bill can close.
  3. Doctors' advice and notes are on paper, so someone types them up later.
Tojo's NoteThe doctor's yes should close the discharge, not start it.
