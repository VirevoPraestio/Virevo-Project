How Tojo sets the priority
HighFix tonight

Tojo got something wrong, or the user said plainly he missed the point.

See what makes it highClose
  • The user says Tojo has not understood.
  • A rating of Bad.
  • The rephrase after a reset also missed.
  • A wrong fact or figure from a reference file.
  • A figure Tojo made up, or one from another hospital.
MediumFix soon

The user did not go along with Tojo, without saying why.

See what makes it mediumClose
  • A rating of Fine.
  • Two misses in a row that set off a reset, where the rephrase then worked.
  • Tojo's question or figure request ignored.
  • A sudden change of subject.
LowWatch

A small slip the user recovered from on their own.

See what makes it lowClose
  • A single ignored question the user answered a turn later.
  • Too long, or the drawing repeats the chat, with no complaint.
  • An unclear word the user asked about once.

Flag each miss as high, medium or low. Send the history and the recommended change, once at the end of the day. A back-end agent reads Tojo's report, adds its own view, works with my team, and updates the files for the next day.

That changes two things from my last answer. Tojo now writes his own suggested changes, not just records. And a back-end agent replaces the nightly review, adding its own view before your team sees anything.

Every miss gets a priority, and nothing leaves the chat during the day. One report goes out at the end of the day, with the full history behind each miss.

  • I'd set end of day at midnight, India time, the same moment the summary pages refresh. Changes your team approves are then in the files Tojo reads the next morning.
