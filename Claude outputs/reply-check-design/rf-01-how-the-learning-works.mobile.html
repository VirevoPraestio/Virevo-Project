What is recorded, and what you receive
Tagged on every turn

What Tojo asked, how the reply was read, and what he built the answer from.

See the tagsClose
  • Tool, place and turn type.
  • What Tojo asked the user for.
  • How the reply was read: answered, ignored, not understood, changed subject, or a rating.
  • The user's reply, shortened, with patient details removed.
  • Which rule sections, playbook step and reference sections he used.
A fixed list of causes

Each miss gets one cause from a fixed list, so they can be counted.

See the causesClose
  • Wrong playbook step, or a step skipped.
  • Misread a reference file or a section of it.
  • Asked the wrong question, or too many.
  • Too long, or the canvas repeated the chat.
  • Jargon or unclear words.
  • Missed what the user actually asked.
Each suggestion you receive

One change to one file, with the proof behind it.

See what it carriesClose
  • The file and section, for example 06 §5.6 or a playbook turn.
  • The words today, and the words suggested.
  • The reason, and the cause it fixes.
  • The chats and turns it comes from, and how many.
  • Whether the rephrase that worked is the example to follow.
  • Whether it applies to one tool or every tool.
Checking it worked

After a change goes in, misses with that cause are counted again.

See how it is checkedClose
  • Misses for that cause, before and after the change.
  • Ratings in the same tool and place, before and after.
  • If misses do not fall, the change comes back to you for another look.

This keeps one person's taste from becoming a rule for everyone.

Noted. Fine and Bad both count as a miss. On a rating turn, a Good lets Tojo answer the waiting message. Fine or Bad sends him back to the rules, references and playbook first, without waiting for a second miss.

The learning works in three layers. Tojo tags every turn as he writes it. A separate review runs every night over all the tags. Only your team can turn a suggestion into a rule.

  • Tojo cannot judge himself fairly in the middle of a chat, so he only records. The nightly review compares many chats and writes the suggestions.
  • A suggestion reaches you only once the same cause shows up in several chats, or once it is serious. One person's habit stays with that person and does not become a rule.
